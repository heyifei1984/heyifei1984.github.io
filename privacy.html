Visitor statistics (GoatCounter)

To see how often pages are read and which links lead here, I use the statistics service GoatCounter. It works without cookies. Your browser loads a small script from gc.zgo.at and reports the visit to heyifei1984.goatcounter.com. Technically, your IP address and browser identification therefore reach GoatCounter's servers; they are used to work out the country and a short-lived session code and are not stored. The script reads the page address, the referring site and your screen width from your browser in order to send them.

  • Recorded: the page visited, the referring site, a rough browser and operating-system type, screen width, language, and the country derived from your IP address. Clicks on the CV download link are counted as an event.
  • Not stored: your IP address, the full browser identification, any personal identifier. To tell repeated page views within a short period from different visitors, GoatCounter keeps a random session code in memory for up to 8 hours; it is not written to disk. The aggregated statistics (no individual data) are kept until I delete them or close the account; I set no automatic deletion date.
  • Not done: no cookies or browser storage for tracking, no profiles, no advertising, no sharing with third parties.

The service is operated by Martin Tournoij (Ireland); its servers are in Finland and Germany (Hetzner). Details: GoatCounter privacy policy. Legal basis is my legitimate interest in knowing how the website is used (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). The website works the same if you block the script in your browser or content blocker.

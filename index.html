About me

I am a cognitive neuroscientist at Philipps University Marburg, based in the Translational Neuroimaging Lab, where I lead externally funded research projects as principal investigator. My research focuses on how predictions and contextual information shape language comprehension, multimodal communication and action perception. I also study social cognition and decision-making.

My research interests include:

  • Language processing: speech perception and sentence comprehension.
  • Multimodal communication: the interaction between gestures and spoken language.
  • Action and perception: sensory prediction and the processing of action outcomes.
  • Social cognition and decision-making: including social exclusion and its behavioural consequences.
  • Clinical research: cognitive and neural alterations in psychiatric conditions, including schizophrenia, depression and tic disorders.

I combine behavioural experiments and computational modelling with EEG, fMRI, simultaneous EEG–fMRI, and non-invasive brain stimulation, including transcranial direct current stimulation (tDCS).

My current projects are funded by the German Research Foundation (DFG) and the von Behring-Röntgen-Stiftung.
